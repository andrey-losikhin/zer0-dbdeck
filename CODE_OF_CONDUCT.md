# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to provide a welcoming, harassment-free environment regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or
expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome different levels of experience,
criticize ideas rather than people, and accept maintainer decisions.
Respect privacy: never publish credentials, personal data, database dumps,
private keys or identifying connection details.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention and
sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to
[andrey.losikhin@gmail.com](mailto:andrey.losikhin@gmail.com).
Do not include private details in public issues. Reports are handled
confidentially as far as reasonably possible. Depending on impact and context,
the maintainer may remove content, reject contributions, warn, temporarily
restrict or permanently ban a participant.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
